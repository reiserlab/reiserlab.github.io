## How other labs do it

Janelia labs: **Aso** (https://www.janelia.org/lab/aso-lab/open-positions) is the most complete: one section per career stage, a qualifications list, "Drosophila experience welcome, but not required", and a tools-available note. **Voigts** (https://www.voigtslab.org/hiring) opens with a values statement and lists qualifications and how to apply. **Branson** (positions page) has an explicit qualifications list addressed to computer scientists. **Dudman** has one warm paragraph plus a contact form under "Our values". **Ahrens** lists inquiry types in one paragraph. Stringer/Pachitariu, Jayaraman, Rubin have no join content.

Non-Janelia: **Steinmetz** (https://www.steinmetzlab.net/a2-join.html) splits postdocs, graduate students, undergraduates, encourages non-traditional backgrounds, and says what undergrads actually get to do. **Datta** (https://datta.hms.harvard.edu/academics/) names the exact materials (cover letter, CV, three references). **Mackenzie Mathis's M-Lab** lists eight applicant categories, exact materials, process limits, and a "last updated" date. **Alexander Mathis** tells applicants to read recent papers first.

Takeaways used in the draft: one short section per career stage; name the exact materials; a three- or four-item "what we look for" list; link only live URLs; show a "links updated" date.
