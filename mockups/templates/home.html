What can fly vision teach us about the brain?

We study the fly visual system to learn where behavior comes from: how a complete wiring diagram, identified neurons and remarkable visual behaviors fit together.

Anatomy

Every lens, every cell

Micro-CT of the eye and electron-microscopy connectomes let us map the whole visual system, one neuron at a time.

Now

From wiring to function

We are adding functional depth to the anatomy, and building robots and AI-accelerated instruments to measure and perturb identified neurons at scale.
